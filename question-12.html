Question:

Charles Osborne (1894-1991) held the Guinness World Record for the longest confirmed attack of hiccups, which lasted this many years.

Hint 1:

This number corresponds to the diameter of the ball in Russian pool.

Hint 2:

Will Smith, Hugh Jackman, Gui Ritchie was born in the year of the answer.

Answer:

68
Osborne's hiccups began after he fell while hanging a 350-pound hog on a farm in Nebraska in 1922. A doctor later said that Osborne may have burst a blood vessel in his brain.
